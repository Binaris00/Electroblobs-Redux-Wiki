# Add-ons List

List of the active add-ons that are available for Redux.

## Grounded Electroblob's Wizardry: Redux

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/2023/90/256/256/639239600176302384.png)

This add-on grounds Electroblob's Wizardry with few things:

1) Now to start casting spells or to bind them - you must first open yourself to Magic through Magic Letter. You can get one by defeating Evil Wizards (20% chance) or Wizards (10% chance), or by trading with one for 9 Diamonds (usual diamonds). - This restriction does not goes onto scrolls. So those are more useful. (In config you can enable feature to bind spells to scrolls without knowledge about magic).

2) To use specific tier of spells - you need to discover at least three of spells of lower tier. As example - to cast Advanced tier magic you must first discover at least three Apprentice level spells.

[Mod Page Link](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/grounded-electroblobs-wizardry-redux)

## Spell Forge for Electroblob's Wizardry: Redux

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/2021/548/256/256/639238810910200494.png)

This mod adds new block - Spell Forge.
Spell Forge is used to create any spell of chosen tier and element.

Also, we introduce new recipes - For all elements of spectral dust, and for arcane tomes!

With Spell Forge you can craft any spell, including ones from add-ons.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1903/772/2026-08-29_12-23-00-png.png)

[Mod Page Link](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/spell-forge-for-electroblobs-wizardry-redux)

## Zetta's Spells

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/1751/613/256/256/639114866632550871.png)

This add-on adds around 10 new spells (as of 1.0.0) and new items!
It expands Necromancer's and Novice-Apprentice level spells experience, adding new spells to Magic element.

Spells:

- Heat Healling (heal with fire!)
- Magic Barrier (do not let evil mobs in!)
- Turn Minion (turn anyone minion!)
- Ore Conversion (convert ores from one ot another!)
- Levitation Sigil (send yourself or them to sky!)
- Advanced Levitation Sigil (even faster!)
- Resurrect (resurrect fallen monsters... but make them on your side!)
- Hex (debuff your enemy, then use Hex to damage them with all debuffs!)
- Summon Rabbit (spawn some rabbit minions, they're Great you might say!)
- Summon Bee (summon BEEs to BEE on your side!)
- Origin (depending on item you hold in other hand - it will affect someone with it, or sneak to apply to yourself!)
- Explosion (explode things!)
- Pull (pull entities to yourself)
- Turn Spellcaster (turn others spellcaster!)
- CREATE OWN SPELLS:

[Mod Page Link](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/zettas-spells)

## Mythical Extension

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/1839/104/256/256/639159122840519846.png)

Mythical Extension is an addon for Electroblob's Wizardry: Redux that adds new content for the later stages of the game. It introduces a new "Mythical" tier of wands and brings new items and mechanics to expand base gameplay.

Features

- New "Mythical" tier wands  
- Player mana system with regeneration  
- Two-handed wands — more powerful, but require an empty off-hand  
- Mythical imbuement altar for crafts
- Updated tooltips showing spells and mana costs  
- New crafting recipes for endgame progression
- Player mana system

## Require Wizard Armor

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/1793/185/256/256/639136031116878166.png)

Adds restriction to allow casting spells only if you wear Wizard armour. 

In config you can switch armor requirement to allow spellcasting only if Armor element matches spell element. Or, for example, require only Hat. Or make required amount of pieces be lower, for example only 3, or 1 piece.

[Mod Page Link](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/require-wizard-armor)

## Wizardry Magic Protection

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/avatars/thumbnails/1902/938/256/256/639188735911117492.png)

What does this mod have to offer? 
- 4 levels of Magic Protection, helping against the many spells in Electroblob's Wizardry Redux
- Just like other protection enchants this is mututally exclusive with others

How would this help me?
- Due to the nature of the spells, just one piece of armor with Magic Protection would be sure to help your survivability
- In PvP contexts this is even more true, helping to bring a more balanced approach to EBWR

[Mod Page Link](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wizardry-magic-protection)
