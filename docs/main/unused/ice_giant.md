# Ice Giant

Ice giants are an elemental representation of cold and fearlessness in the form of a big ice golem, normally created accidentally by using [ice](/main/extra/element) spells without the needed knowledge or by using the "summon ice giant" spell.

## Behavior

Depending on the cast context, the ice giant could be driven towards attacking hostile mobs, or, if created by evil forces, it will attack players and [wizards](/main/entity/wizard). It will try to get near the target to knock it into the air by his arms, giving Slowness I to its targets in the process.
