# Shadow Wraith

Shadow wraiths are the dark apparitions in the world, creating chaos and increasing the evil in all the creatures. They can be created accidentally by using [necromancy](/main/extra/element) spells that you can't control in your level. It can also be summoned by players and other spell casters using the "summon shadow wraith" spell.

## Behavior

Shadow wraiths behave similarly to blazes, but instead of fireballs, they shoot dark orbs at their enemies. They can fly, and, unlike blazes, they won't take damage when wet.

When detecting a target, a shadow wraith will fly into the air and start the attack with a series of 1-2 shots, after that, it will descend and wait for a short cooldown to continue with the attack.
