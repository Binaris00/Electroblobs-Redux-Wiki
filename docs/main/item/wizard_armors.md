# Wizard Armors

Wizard armor is a form of magical armor which uses mana to shield its wearer from incoming damage. Both regular and elemental wizard armor sets are available, though only the former can be crafted. Each set consists of a hat, robes, leggings and boots. Wizard armor may also be found in loot chests or purchased from [wizards](/main/entity/wizard).

<img src="/resources/img/item/wizard_armors.png" class="full-mc-icon"/>

## Appearance

Wizard armor has a cloth-like appearance, with a base color and a highlight color which depend on the [element](/main/extra/element). Wizard armor has a custom model: wizard hats are pointed with a wide brim and folded-over peak, wizard robes extend downwards over the player's legs, and wizard leggings and boots are similar in shape to the armor in vanilla _Minecraft_.

![Wizard armor on stand](/resources/img/screenshots/wizard_armor_on_stand.png)

## Obtaining

Regular, non-elemental wizard armor may be obtained by crafting it from [magical silk](/main/item/magical_silk). All wizard armor may be obtained from loot chests, with an equal chance for items from each of the eight sets. Wizard armor of all types may also be purchased from [wizards](/main/entity/wizard). All wizards and [evil wizards](/main/entity/evil_wizard) wear wizard armor, and evil wizards will occasionally drop one or more of their armor pieces as a rare drop.

## Usage

When worn, wizard armor provides protection between that of chain armor and iron armor. Wizard armor consumes mana in the same way as other armor consumes durability - the amount of mana consumed depends on the amount of damage and type of incoming attacks. However, when wizard armor reaches zero mana, instead of breaking it becomes depleted, remaining in the armor slot but no longer providing any protection. Wizard armor may be recharged by placing it in the central slot of an [arcane workbench](/main/block/arcane_workbench) and supplying it with [magic crystals](/main/item/magic_crystal) in the same way as with a [wand](/main/item/wands).

Elemental wizard armor provides its wearer with a cost reduction for spells of the same element of 10% per armor piece.

Wizard armor may also be enchanted at an enchanting table. It has an enchantability of 15, equal to that of leather armor.

## Upgrades

A wizard armor could be upgraded to 3 different types in an [arcane workbench](/main/block/arcane_workbench), each one having new bonus effects and uses in the world of magic.

You can find these upgrades searching structures around the world

<img src="/resources/img/item/armor_upgrades.png" class="full-mc-icon"/>

### Battlemage

More focused on melee magic combat and quick battles. Gives 5% cooldown reduction and 5% mana cost reduction for the armor's element. The full set effect gives [Ward](/main/mob_effect/ward) I

### Sage

Intense study in your main element drives you into this, learning the deep fundamentals and how your magic interacts with the world. Gives more enchantability to your armor compared to the other types, with a 20% mana cost reduction for the armor's element. The full set gives another 20% of mana reduction for other elements.

### Warlock

Used to drive magic into intense combat, focusing on creating chaos and fast destruction in war. Gives 10% of cooldown reduction. The full set effect gives +20% movement speed.
