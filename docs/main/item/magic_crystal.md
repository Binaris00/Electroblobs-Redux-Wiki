# Magic Crystal

<img src="/resources/img/item/crystals.png" class="full-mc-icon"/>

Magic crystals are the physical source of mana used to charge [wands](/main/item/wands) and [wizard armor](/main/item/wizard_armors), as well as being an ingredient in numerous crafting recipes. They are central to wizardry's gameplay, and in fact the first step in wizardry is to gather magic crystals.

## Obtaining

Magic crystals drop from [crystal ore](/main/block/crystal_ore) when mined, which is their primary source. Smelting crystal ore in a furnace yields a single magic crystal. They can also be crafted from [crystal flowers](/main/block/crystal_flower), which can be found growing on grass at the surface of the overworld, and from [crystal blocks](/main/block/crystal_block), which are their storage block counterpart. Magic crystals are also dropped by [evil wizards](/main/entity/evil_wizard) when killed, and can be obtained by trading any spell book with a wizard. Additionally, they can be found as loot in dungeon chests and some [wizard towers](/main/structure/wizard_tower).

## Usage

Magic crystals are used primarily to charge wands and wizard armor in the [arcane workbench](/main/block/arcane_workbench) or via [mana flasks](/main/item/mana_flask). They are also used in most of wizardry's crafting recipes, including being crafted with a book to obtain The Wizard's Handbook. All wizard trades (except exchanging a spell book for crystals) cost some number of magic crystals along with another trading item (like gold).
