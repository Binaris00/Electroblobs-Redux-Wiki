# Storm Elemental

Storm elemental are the elemental representation for storms and thunders, creating and manifesting dangerous events in the world. They can be created accidentally by using [lightning](/main/extra/element) spells that you can't control in your level. It can also be summoned by players and other spell casters using the "summon storm elemental" spell.

## Behavior

Storm elementals behave similarly to blazes, but instead of fireballs, they shoot lightning disc to their enemies. They can fly, and, unlike blazes, they won't take damage when wet.

When detecting a target, a storm elemental will fly into the air and start the attack with a series of 3 shots, after that, it will descend and wait for a short cooldown to continue with the attack.
