## Description

Font of mana is a beneficial status effect which dramatically reduces a player's [spell](/main/spells) cooldowns. Font of mana I reduce spell cooldowns to 1/2 of their normal duration, font of mana II reduces them to 1/3 of their normal duration, and so on (though amplifiers greater than II can only be attained via commands).

## Sources

The font of mana spell grants the caster font of mana I, or, when used on a master healing [wand](/main/item/wands), font of mana II. The only other source of the effect is via commands.

## Trivia

- The font of mana effect stacks with [wand cooldown upgrades](/main/item/wand_upgrades).
