# Spells

> **Spoiler alert!** The information in the table below may spoil your enjoyment of the mod, as it contains details of spells you may not have discovered. I recommend playing the mod for a while before reading about all the spells; discovering spells you have never seen before is a big part of the gameplay. If you have played the mod, or don't really mind spoilers, then read on...

### Key to headings

- **#** The order of the spells as shown in the creative tab
- **Icon** The spell's icon as displayed in its spell book and on the spell HUD
- **Name** The name of the spell in English
- **Tier** The tier that the spell belongs to
- **Element** The element that the spell belongs to
- **Type** The type of spell - attack, defense, utility, etc.
- **Mana** The mana cost of the spell
- **Continuous?** Whether the spell requires the use item button to be held in order to cast it
- **Wizards?** Whether wizard NPCs can cast this spell
