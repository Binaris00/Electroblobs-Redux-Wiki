# Receptacle

Receptacles are small essence holders that serve the purpose of containing [spectral dust](/main/item/spectral_dust) inside them, glowing depending on the spectral dust element.

## Obtaining

You can find some receptacles encrusted with an [imbuement altar](/main/block/imbuement_altar) inside [library ruins](/main/structure/library_ruins). It could also be obtained by crafting it from some gold ingots and a flower pot:

## Usage

When you use a spectral dust with a receptacle, this one will save the essence for you, giving a small source of light. This could also be used with the imbuement altar to provide fuel to powerful magic recipes.

You can place a receptacle on a normal surface or affixed to a wall.
